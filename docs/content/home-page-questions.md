## Known facts (iltan, 2026-11-13)

- **Size:** about 140 members.
- **Rides:** a big ride/event every week when the weather allows (in bad weather few people join, so it is
  effectively cancelled). Besides that, small daily meet-ups: 3–8 members who live nearby meet at a café and chat.
- **Where:** meet-ups on both the Asian and the European side, usually near the sea (e.g. Beşiktaş, Üsküdar,
  Kadıköy, Beykoz), but not always.
- **Who can join:** anyone aged 18 or over. Any licence and bike: some ride a 50cc with a B licence, others a
  700–1000cc with an A licence. New riders and women are welcome. Planned: some events only for certain bike classes
  (e.g. over 400cc).
- **Joining:** apply → if approved, "ön alım" (a few days of evaluation in a separate group) → moved into the main
  group.
- **Cost:** membership is free. Paid stops (e.g. a breakfast on an event) are paid by each person; sometimes there is
  a group discount (not promised).
- **Rules:** obey the traffic rules, be kind, no swearing, be respectful — members span a wide age range, many are
  married/families.
