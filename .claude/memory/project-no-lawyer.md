The club will **not** consult a lawyer for KVKK / privacy-notice questions (iltan, 2026-11-13): it costs a lot and
isn't needed. Claude researches and gives the final decision on the open "lawyer questions" later.

**Why:** cost; an informal club of ~150 members.

**How to apply:** never park an item as "ask the lawyer"; research official sources (KVKK Board decisions, the law
text) and propose a decision. Older docs (`docs/PLAN.md` KVKK section, `docs/follow-ups.md` "lawyer questions")
still say "lawyer"; treat those as Claude's to decide. Related: [[project-ridingclub]].
