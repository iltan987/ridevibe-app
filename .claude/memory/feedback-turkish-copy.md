When writing Turkish UI/public copy, check each word's everyday connotation, not just its dictionary meaning, and
leave out details that state the obvious or sound transactional.

Rejected by iltan (2026-11-13, home-page copy): "hafta içi kahvede" (kahve = old men's tea house), "sohbette"
(religious "sohbet" gatherings where a hoca talks), "ücretsiz üyelik" (every riding club is free; obvious),
"moladaki kahvaltıyı herkes kendi öder" (sounds like "we won't pay for you"; unnecessary).

**Why:** copy that sounds translated or carries an odd connotation makes the club look off to native readers.
**How to apply:** prefer plain, warm, natural phrasing ("bir arada", "buluşup muhabbet ediyoruz"); when a word has a
second social/religious/class meaning, pick another; offer 2–3 alternatives for headlines and let iltan pick. See
[[project-ridingclub]].
